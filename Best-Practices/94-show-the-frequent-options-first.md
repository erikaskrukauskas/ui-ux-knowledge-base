# Show the frequent options first

Show the few options most people need. Reveal the rare ones when someone asks. The path to the rare options is visible and labeled with what they will find.

## When it applies

Use this when a view has both everyday choices and specialist ones.

## The decision

- Put frequent options on the first view.
- Put rare options one request away, with a label that says what is there.
- Stop at two levels. A third level is where people get lost.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 3 December 2006: "Progressive disclosure defers advanced or rarely used features to a secondary screen, making applications easier to learn and less error-prone." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Show the frequent options first](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/).
