# Title the view by its purpose

Each view has a title that states its topic or purpose.

## When it applies

Use this for a page, a screen, or a document the person opens.

## The decision

- Name the purpose, not only the product.
- Put the distinctive words first so a list of views is scannable.
- Update the title when the view changes.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Web pages have titles that describe topic or purpose." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Title the view by its purpose](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#page-titled).
