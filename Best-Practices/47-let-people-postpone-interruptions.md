# Let people postpone interruptions

The person can postpone or suppress interruptions, except an emergency.

## When it applies

Use this when the stronger interruptions criterion is in scope, and whenever a notification can wait.

## The decision

- Let the person put off or silence non-emergency interruptions.
- An emergency may still interrupt. Name what counts as an emergency for this product and confirm the criterion.
- A marketing prompt is not an emergency.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Interruptions can be postponed or suppressed by the user, except interruptions involving an emergency." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people postpone interruptions](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#interruptions).
