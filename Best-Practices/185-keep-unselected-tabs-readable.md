# Keep unselected tabs readable

Unselected tabs stay visible and readable, so the other choices can be seen.

## When it applies

Use this for every tab that is not selected.

## The decision

- Keep enough contrast to read the label.
- Do not fade a tab until it looks unavailable.
- A tab the person cannot see will not be opened.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Unselected tabs should be visible to remind users of the additional options." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep unselected tabs readable](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
