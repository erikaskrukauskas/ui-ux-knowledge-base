# Let people check any submission

Any view that submits information is reversible, checked with a chance to correct, or confirmed before it is final. This is the stronger prevention rule, beyond legal and payment flows.

## When it applies

Use this when that criterion is in scope.

## The decision

- Make the submission reversible, or
- Detect input errors and let the person correct them, or
- Let them review, confirm, and correct before the submit is final.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "For web pages that require the user to submit information, at least one of the following is true." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people check any submission](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#error-prevention-all).
