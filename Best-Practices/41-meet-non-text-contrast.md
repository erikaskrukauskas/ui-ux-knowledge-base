# Meet non-text contrast

The boundary, icon, or state graphic a person needs in order to identify a control has at least 3:1 contrast against the adjacent color. The same ratio applies to parts of a graphic required to understand it.

## When it applies

Use this for input borders, icons that mean something, and selected states. Inactive controls and untouched user-agent controls have the exceptions in the criterion.

## The decision

- Check the part that shows where the control is and whether it is selected.
- A graphic that carries meaning uses the same 3:1 rule for the parts that carry that meaning.
- Confirm the current ratio and exceptions. Text contrast is a different criterion.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "The visual presentation of the following have a contrast ratio of at least 3:1 against adjacent color(s)." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Meet non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#non-text-contrast).
