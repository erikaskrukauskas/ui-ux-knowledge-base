# Let people choose orientation

The task works in both portrait and landscape unless a single orientation is essential to the activity.

## When it applies

Use this for views the person may rotate. A check deposit, a piano, a slide, or a virtual-reality view can be the essential exception. Confirm that exception.

## The decision

- Support both orientations for an ordinary form, list, or article.
- Keep the same actions available after rotation.
- If you lock orientation, record why it is essential and confirm the current exception.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Content does not restrict its view and operation to a single display orientation, such as portrait or landscape, unless a specific display orientation is essential." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people choose orientation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#orientation).
