# Mark the selected tab in more than one way

The selected tab is marked by at least two cues, so the selection is obvious.

## When it applies

Use this for every tab list, and especially when there are only two tabs.

## The decision

- Combine cues such as weight, a line, and a shared region.
- Do not rely on a thin line or a slight color shift.
- The current tab is also named in text, not only styled.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Use at least two selection indicators to enhance the visual salience of the selected tab." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Mark the selected tab in more than one way](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
