# Caption prerecorded speech

Prerecorded video that includes audio has captions synchronized with the speech and other meaningful sounds.

## When it applies

Use this for recorded media. A clip that is a labeled alternative for text on the page is the exception named in the criterion.

## The decision

- Caption speech, speaker changes, and sounds that matter to the task.
- Keep the captions synchronized with the media.
- Do not rely on a transcript alone when the person is watching the video.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Captions are provided for all prerecorded audio content in synchronized media, except when the media is a media alternative for text and is clearly labeled as such." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Caption prerecorded speech](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#captions-prerecorded).
