# Describe or replace prerecorded video

Prerecorded video in synchronized media has an audio description or a full time-based alternative, unless the video is a labeled alternative for text.

## When it applies

Use this when the picture carries information the soundtrack does not.

## The decision

- Describe the visual information the person needs, in sync with the video, or provide a full alternative.
- Include actions, on-screen text, and scene changes that change the meaning.
- A later criterion may require audio description specifically. Confirm the level you are meeting.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "An alternative for time-based media or audio description of the prerecorded video content is provided for synchronized media, except when the media is a media alternative for text and is clearly labeled as such." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Describe or replace prerecorded video](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#audio-description-or-media-alternative-prerecorded).
