# Offer an alternative for audio or silent video

Prerecorded audio with no video, and prerecorded video with no audio, come with an equivalent alternative.

## When it applies

Use this when the recording is the information, not a labeled alternative to text already on the view.

## The decision

- For audio only, provide a text alternative that carries the same information.
- For silent video, provide that alternative or an audio track that carries the same information.
- Label a clip that is itself the alternative for text already on the page.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "An alternative for time-based media is provided that presents equivalent information for prerecorded audio-only content." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer an alternative for audio or silent video](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#audio-only-and-video-only-prerecorded).
