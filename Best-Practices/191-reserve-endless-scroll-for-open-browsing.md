# Reserve endless scroll for open browsing

Endless scroll fits a stream of similar items with no particular item to find. A search, a comparison, or a short look at the top needs pages or an explicit load.

## When it applies

Use this when you choose how a long list grows.

## The decision

- Use endless scroll for browsing with no target, such as a news or entertainment stream.
- Do not use it when the person is looking for one item, comparing distant items, or reading only the top results.
- Offer pages or a load-more action when they must return, compare, or reach the end.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 4 September 2022: "Infinite scrolling typically works best for situations where users will want to scroll through homogeneous items with no particular task or goal in mind." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Reserve endless scroll for open browsing](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/infinite-scrolling-tips/).
