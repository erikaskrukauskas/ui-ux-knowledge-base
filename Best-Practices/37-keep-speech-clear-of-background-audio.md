# Keep speech clear of background audio

Prerecorded speech that is the main content has no background, a way to turn the background off, or background that is substantially quieter than the speech. This is the stronger audio criterion.

## When it applies

Use this for a spoken lesson, a story, or an announcement. Music, an audio logo, and an audio CAPTCHA are outside it.

## The decision

- Prefer a speech track without background sound.
- If background remains, let the person turn it off, or keep it at least 20 decibels below the speech.
- Confirm the current decibel rule and the exceptions before you rely on the number.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "The background sounds are at least 20 decibels lower than the foreground speech content, with the exception of occasional sounds that last for only one or two seconds." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep speech clear of background audio](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#low-or-no-background-audio).
