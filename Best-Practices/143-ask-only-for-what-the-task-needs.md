# Ask only for what the task needs

A form asks for what the transaction needs. Extra questions make people leave.

## When it applies

Use this when you add a field.

## The decision

- Drop a fact you can derive, collect later, or omit.
- Keep optional questions few, and name them optional.
- Every field you cut is a field the person does not have to understand.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 1 May 2016: "Remove fields which collect information that can be (a) derived in some other way, (b) collected more conveniently at a later date, or (c) simply omitted." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Ask only for what the task needs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/).
