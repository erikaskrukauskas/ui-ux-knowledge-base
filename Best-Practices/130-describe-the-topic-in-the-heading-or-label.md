# Describe the topic in the heading or label

A heading or a label says what that section or control is for.

## When it applies

Use this whenever a heading or a label is shown.

## The decision

- Make it descriptive enough to predict the section or the expected input.
- A short label is enough when the cue is clear.
- An accurate name still fails if it is vague, even when the structure is exposed.

## Evidence

W3C, Understanding Success Criterion 2.4.6 Headings and Labels: "Headings and labels describe topic or purpose." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Describe the topic in the heading or label](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html).
