# Use one column for a form

Fields sit in one column, one row each, so the person keeps moving down the form.

## When it applies

Use this for a form longer than a couple of fields.

## The decision

- Give each field its own row.
- Put only a short related set, such as a city and a region, on one row.
- Do not make the person restart their scan at the top of a second column.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 1 May 2016: "Multiple columns interrupt the vertical momentum of moving down the form." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Use one column for a form](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/).
