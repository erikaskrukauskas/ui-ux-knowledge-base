# Report whether the submission worked

After a submission, say whether it succeeded or what failed.

## When it applies

Use this when the person sends a form.

## The decision

- Confirm success in the heading or the title.
- On failure, say that there are errors and how many.
- Do not leave the view unchanged and silent.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, User Notification: "Provide feedback to users about the results of their form submission, whether successful or not." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Report whether the submission worked](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/).
