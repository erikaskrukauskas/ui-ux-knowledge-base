# Pair a valid or invalid cue with text

A checkmark, a border, or a color is accompanied by text that says the field is valid or what to fix.

## When it applies

Use this for inline success and error on a field.

## The decision

- Show text, not only a color or an icon.
- Tell them how to correct an error.
- Keep their entry in the field.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, User Notification: "Typically a combination of messages and visual cues are used to provide in-line feedback." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Pair a valid or invalid cue with text](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/notifications/).
