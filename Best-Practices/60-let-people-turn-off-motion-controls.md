# Let people turn off motion controls

A function triggered by moving the device or the person also has an on-screen control, and the motion trigger can be turned off, unless the motion is essential.

## When it applies

Use this for shake, tilt, or raise-to-act. This is separate from decorative animation.

## The decision

- Provide a control that does the same thing without motion.
- Let the person disable the motion response so an accidental movement does not fire it.
- Confirm the essential exception, for example a pedometer, before you require motion.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Functionality that can be operated by device motion or user motion can also be operated by user interface components and responding to the motion can be disabled to prevent accidental actuation." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people turn off motion controls](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#motion-actuation).
