# Keep needed information on the step

Information required to finish the step is visible on the step. The person does not have to remember it from an earlier view.

## When it applies

Use this when a later step depends on an earlier choice, a code, or a price.

## The decision

- Show the fact they need where they decide.
- Carry forward a value they already entered instead of asking them to remember it.
- Help beside the field beats a lesson they were asked to memorize.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 30 January 2024: "The user should not have to remember information from one part of the interface to another." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep needed information on the step](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/).
