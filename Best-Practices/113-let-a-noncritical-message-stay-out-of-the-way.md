# Let a noncritical message stay out of the way

A message that does not require an immediate decision leaves the main task usable. The person can ignore it, move it, or dismiss it without stopping.

## When it applies

Use this for a status, a hint, or a notice that can wait.

## The decision

- Do not disable the task for a message they may ignore.
- Do not cover the information they need in order to continue.
- Switch to an interruption only when the task cannot safely continue.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 23 April 2017: "In contrast, nonmodal (or modeless) dialogs and windows do not disable the main content: showing the dialog box doesn’t change the functionality of the user interface." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let a noncritical message stay out of the way](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/modal-nonmodal-dialog/).
