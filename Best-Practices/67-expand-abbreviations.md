# Expand abbreviations

An abbreviation has a way to find its expanded form or meaning.

## When it applies

Use this the first time an abbreviation carries meaning the person needs.

## The decision

- Expand it in place or provide the expansion on request.
- Use the same expansion throughout the flow.
- If the letters are the name people already know, confirm whether the criterion still wants an expansion.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "A mechanism for identifying the expanded form or meaning of abbreviations is available." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Expand abbreviations](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#abbreviations).
