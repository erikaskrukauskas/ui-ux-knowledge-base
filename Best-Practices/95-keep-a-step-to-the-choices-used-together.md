# Keep a step to the choices used together

When a task is a sequence, each step shows the choices used together at that moment. Choices used only later wait for the later step. Choices the person compares stay together.

## When it applies

Use this when one screen is either too crowded or split so the person cannot compare.

## The decision

- Group options that are adjusted together.
- Defer details that belong to a later step, such as payment during an exploration.
- Do not split a comparison across pages the person must remember.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 3 December 2006: "Staged disclosure is useful when you can divide a task into distinct steps that have little interaction. It is problematic when the steps are interdependent and users must alternate between them." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep a step to the choices used together](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/).
