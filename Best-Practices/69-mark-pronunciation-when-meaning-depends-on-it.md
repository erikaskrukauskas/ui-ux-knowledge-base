# Mark pronunciation when meaning depends on it

When a word's meaning is ambiguous unless the person knows how it is pronounced, provide that pronunciation.

## When it applies

Use this for the rare word that changes meaning with pronunciation, not for every name.

## The decision

- Provide the pronunciation where the ambiguity is.
- Leave ordinary words unmarked.
- Confirm the current criterion before you add pronunciation to a whole article.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "A mechanism is available for identifying specific pronunciation of words where meaning of the words, in context, is ambiguous without knowing the pronunciation." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Mark pronunciation when meaning depends on it](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#pronunciation).
