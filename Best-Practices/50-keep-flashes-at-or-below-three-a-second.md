# Keep flashes at or below three a second

Nothing flashes more than three times in one second, unless the flash is below the general and red flash thresholds.

## When it applies

Use this for animation, video, and large transitions.

## The decision

- Do not flash content more than three times in any one second.
- If a flash is claimed to be below the threshold, confirm the current threshold definition. Do not guess it.
- This applies to the whole view, because a flash can block the rest of the task.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Web pages do not contain anything that flashes more than three times in any one second period, or the flash is below the general flash and red flash thresholds." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep flashes at or below three a second](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#three-flashes-or-below-threshold).
