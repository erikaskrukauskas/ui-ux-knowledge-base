# Show whether a submenu is open

A menu item that has a submenu shows that fact, and shows whether the submenu is open.

## When it applies

Use this for a drop-down or fly-out in navigation.

## The decision

- Show an indicator people can see.
- Expose the expanded or collapsed state.
- Update the state when the submenu opens or closes.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Fly-out Menus: "Indicate navigation menu items with submenus visually and using markup." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Show whether a submenu is open](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/menus/flyout/).
