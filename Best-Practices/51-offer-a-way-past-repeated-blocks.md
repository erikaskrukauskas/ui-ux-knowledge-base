# Offer a way past repeated blocks

A person can skip blocks that repeat on every view, such as a long navigation, and go to the content of this view.

## When it applies

Use this when the same large block precedes the task on many views.

## The decision

- Provide a skip control or another mechanism that bypasses the repeated block.
- Put it where keyboard and screen-reader users meet it first.
- The destination is the start of the unique content, not another menu.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "A mechanism is available to bypass blocks of content that are repeated on multiple web pages." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer a way past repeated blocks](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#bypass-blocks).
