# Offer sign language for prerecorded audio

Prerecorded audio in synchronized media can include a sign-language interpretation. This is an additional requirement beyond captions, for when that level is in scope.

## When it applies

Use this when the product commits to the sign-language criterion, or when the audience depends on sign language.

## The decision

- Provide sign language for the prerecorded audio content.
- Keep it synchronized and visible without covering the captions.
- Confirm the current criterion before you treat captions as a substitute.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Sign language interpretation is provided for all prerecorded audio content in synchronized media." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer sign language for prerecorded audio](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#sign-language-prerecorded).
