# Associate the label with that field

The visible label is tied to its field, so the name and the larger target point at the same control.

## When it applies

Use this for every field you label.

## The decision

- Prefer an explicit association between the label and the field.
- Wrap the label around the field only when you cannot tie them explicitly.
- Do not rely on a tooltip as the name.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Labeling Controls: "Generally, explicit labels are better supported by assistive technology." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Associate the label with that field](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/).
