# Let people review a consequential submission

A submission that creates a legal commitment, moves money, changes or deletes stored data, or sends test answers is reversible, checked for errors with a chance to fix them, or confirmed on a review step.

## When it applies

Use this before the person is committed. A delete confirmation is a related, narrower rule.

## The decision

- Let them reverse the submission, or
- Check the input and let them correct it, or
- Show a review where they can confirm or correct before the final submit.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "For web pages that cause legal commitments or financial transactions for the user to occur, that modify or delete user-controllable data in data storage systems, or that submit user test responses, at least one of the following is true." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people review a consequential submission](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#error-prevention-legal-financial-data).
