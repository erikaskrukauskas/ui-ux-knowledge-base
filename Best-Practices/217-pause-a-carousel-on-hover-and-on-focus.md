# Pause a carousel on hover and on focus

A moving carousel pauses while the pointer is over it or while it has keyboard focus.

## When it applies

Use this when slides advance on their own.

## The decision

- Pause on hover so a person can read and use a link.
- Pause on keyboard focus so they do not lose their place.
- Keep a separate stop control as well.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Carousel Animations: "Pause the carousel animation when the mouse pointer is hovering over the carousel or when it receives keyboard focus." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Pause a carousel on hover and on focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/animations/).
