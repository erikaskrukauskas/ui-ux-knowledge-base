# Let people stop autoplaying audio

Audio that plays by itself for more than three seconds can be paused, stopped, or turned down without changing the system volume.

## When it applies

Use this when a view starts sound on its own.

## The decision

- Provide a control to pause or stop the audio, or a volume control for that audio alone.
- Put the control early in the focus order so a screen-reader user can reach it.
- Confirm the current three-second threshold before you rely on the number.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "If any audio on a web page plays automatically for more than 3 seconds, either a mechanism is available to pause or stop the audio, or a mechanism is available to control audio volume independently from the overall system volume level." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people stop autoplaying audio](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#audio-control).
