# Do not skip heading ranks

Heading ranks nest. Do not jump from a section to a subsection two levels down.

## When it applies

Use this when a view has more than one level of heading.

## The decision

- Start the next subsection one rank below its parent.
- Skipping back up, to close a subsection, is fine.
- Keep the visible importance and the rank in agreement.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Headings: "Skipping heading ranks can be confusing and should be avoided where possible." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Do not skip heading ranks](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/page-structure/headings/).
