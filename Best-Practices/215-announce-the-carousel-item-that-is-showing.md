# Announce the carousel item that is showing

When the slide changes, say which item is showing and how many there are.

## When it applies

Use this whenever a carousel moves to another item.

## The decision

- Announce the position, such as item 2 of 5.
- Do not rely on the picture changing.
- Offer a way to open any item directly, and mark the current one in text.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Carousel Functionality: "Changes to carousel items must be communicated to all users, including screen reader users." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Announce the carousel item that is showing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/).
