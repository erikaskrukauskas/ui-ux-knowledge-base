# Tell the person before a consequential action

An action with consequences for the person is not taken in silence. They learn what the system is about to do, or what it just did, in time to respond.

## When it applies

Use this for save, send, pay, delete, and any change they cannot see on the control itself.

## The decision

- Say what will happen before a consequential action, or what did happen immediately after.
- A silent success that looks unchanged is not informed consent to the result.
- Pair the notice with the state for that action.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 30 January 2024: "Communicate clearly to users what the system’s state is — no action with consequences to users should be taken without informing them." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Tell the person before a consequential action](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/).
