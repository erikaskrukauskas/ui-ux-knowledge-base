# Show the hierarchy in the breadcrumb

A breadcrumb shows the parent path in the structure, not the pages visited in this session.

## When it applies

Use this on a deep view that has real ancestors.

## The decision

- List the ancestors back toward the start of the hierarchy.
- Do not list the session history. That trail gets long, repeats, and fails for someone who arrived directly.
- The back action is a different control.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 1 September 2026: "Breadcrumbs are not intended to show the history of pages traversed during a session on the site; they are intended to show the hierarchical structure of the site." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Show the hierarchy in the breadcrumb](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/breadcrumbs/).
