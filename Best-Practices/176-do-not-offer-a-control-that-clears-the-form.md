# Do not offer a control that clears the form

A form does not include a control that erases everything the person typed.

## When it applies

Use this at the end of a form.

## The decision

- Offer submit, and a quiet cancel only when they must be able to discard sensitive input.
- Do not put Clear or Reset next to the fields.
- The risk of wiping the form outweighs the rare wish to start over.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 1 May 2016: "The risk of accidental deletion outweighs the unlikely need to ‘start over’ on a web form." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Do not offer a control that clears the form](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/).
