# Mark a passage in another language

A passage or phrase in a different language is marked as that language. Proper names, technical terms, and words that have joined the surrounding language are the exceptions.

## When it applies

Use this when a sentence switches language.

## The decision

- Mark the language of the passage.
- Leave a proper name in the surrounding language mark.
- Confirm the current exceptions before you mark every foreign-looking word.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "The human language of each passage or phrase in the content can be programmatically determined except for proper names, technical terms, words of indeterminate language, and words or phrases that have become part of the vernacular of the immediately surrounding text." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Mark a passage in another language](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#language-of-parts).
