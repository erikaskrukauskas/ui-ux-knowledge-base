# Caption live speech

Live audio in synchronized media has captions.

## When it applies

Use this for a live stream, a live event, or any audio that is happening as the person watches.

## The decision

- Provide captions for the live speech.
- Keep them close enough in time that the person can follow the event.
- Confirm the current criterion before treating a delayed transcript as enough.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Captions are provided for all live audio content in synchronized media." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Caption live speech](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#captions-live).
