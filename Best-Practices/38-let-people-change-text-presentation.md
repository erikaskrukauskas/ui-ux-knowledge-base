# Let people change text presentation

For blocks of text, the person can select colors, keep the line from becoming too long, avoid full justification, increase spacing, and resize text without horizontal scrolling. The browser may provide the mechanism.

## When it applies

Use this when you present paragraphs. Confirm the current measurements before you treat them as the only legal layout.

## The decision

- Do not lock colors, justification, or line length so the person cannot override them.
- The criterion currently names 80 characters, space-and-a-half line spacing, and 200 percent resize without horizontal scrolling.
- Writing systems that do not use one of these settings are not forced to invent it. Confirm that note.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "For the visual presentation of blocks of text, a mechanism is available to achieve the following." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people change text presentation](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#visual-presentation).
