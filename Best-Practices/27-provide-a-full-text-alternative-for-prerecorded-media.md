# Provide a full text alternative for prerecorded media

Prerecorded synchronized media and prerecorded silent video have a full time-based alternative when that stronger criterion is in scope.

## When it applies

Use this when a description track is not enough and the person needs the whole story in text.

## The decision

- Write an alternative that includes dialogue, meaningful sounds, and visual information in order.
- Offer it with the media.
- Confirm whether your conformance level requires this in addition to captions and description.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "An alternative for time-based media is provided for all prerecorded synchronized media and for all prerecorded video-only media." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Provide a full text alternative for prerecorded media](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#media-alternative-prerecorded).
