# Let people skip an optional stage

An optional stage is recognizable and skippable.

## When it applies

Use this when a step does not apply to everyone.

## The decision

- Say that the stage is optional in the heading.
- Offer a way to skip it.
- Do not make people discover it is optional by filling it in.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Multi-page Forms: "Make it easy to recognize and to skip optional stages." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people skip an optional stage](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/).
