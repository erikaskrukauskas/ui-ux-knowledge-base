# Keep every edge of focus visible

Under the stronger focus criterion, no part of the focused control is hidden by author-created content.

## When it applies

Use this when that criterion is in scope. The minimum criterion only forbids hiding the control entirely.

## The decision

- Keep the whole focused control visible.
- Do not let a sticky bar cover the edge of the focus indicator.
- Confirm the current wording before you treat a partly covered control as passing.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "When a user interface component receives keyboard focus, no part of the component is hidden by author-created content." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep every edge of focus visible](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-not-obscured-enhanced).
