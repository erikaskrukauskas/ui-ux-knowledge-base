# Say how many steps will follow

The first step says how many steps are coming, and each later step says how far the person is.

## When it applies

Use this for a process with a known sequence.

## The decision

- Tell the count at the start.
- Update the count when answers change how many steps remain.
- When the count can only be approximate, say so.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Multi-page Forms: "If possible, the first step of a form should explain how many steps will follow." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Say how many steps will follow](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/).
