# Teach with a tip on the step

Teach the action on the step where it is used. A general tour at startup asks the person to memorize a lesson they cannot yet practice.

## When it applies

Use this the first time a gesture or a rare control appears.

## The decision

- Put a short tip next to the control.
- Dismiss the tip without blocking the task.
- Leave the label in place after the tip is gone.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 15 January 2024: "Instead of showing general tutorials, use contextual tips tailored to the page that the user is visiting." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Teach with a tip on the step](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/).
