# Let people authenticate without a puzzle

Signing in does not depend on a memory or puzzle test unless there is another method, help to complete the test, or the test is object recognition or personal content the person supplied.

## When it applies

Use this for passwords, codes, and CAPTCHA-like steps.

## The decision

- Allow a password manager and paste.
- Offer a method that does not require recalling a secret unaided, when you can.
- Confirm the current exceptions, including object recognition and personal content, before you keep a puzzle.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "A cognitive function test (such as remembering a password or solving a puzzle) is not required for any step in an authentication process unless that step provides at least one of the following." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people authenticate without a puzzle](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#accessible-authentication-minimum).
