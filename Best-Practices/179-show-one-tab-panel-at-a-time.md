# Show one tab panel at a time

A tab shows one panel from a list. Selecting the label shows that panel.

## When it applies

Use this when related content splits into a few peer groups.

## The decision

- Show the list, a short label, the selected state, and one panel.
- Selecting a tab replaces the panel. It does not stack the panels.
- If the person must see two groups at once, do not use tabs.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Tabs allow users to selectively view a single panel of content from among a list of options." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Show one tab panel at a time](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
