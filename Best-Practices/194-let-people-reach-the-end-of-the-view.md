# Let people reach the end of the view

A list that never ends must not block the footer or the last action on the view.

## When it applies

Use this when content keeps loading as the person scrolls.

## The decision

- Stop the stream so they can reach what comes after it.
- A load-more control leaves the end of the view reachable.
- Do not hide contact, policy, or other end matter under an endless stream.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 4 September 2022: "Infinite scrolling (without a Load More button) can make it impossible to access the footer of a website." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people reach the end of the view](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/infinite-scrolling-tips/).
