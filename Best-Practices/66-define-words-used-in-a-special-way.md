# Define words used in a special way

A word or phrase used in an unusual or restricted way, including jargon and idioms, has a way to find its specific definition.

## When it applies

Use this when a term does not mean what the person would expect.

## The decision

- Link or otherwise provide the definition where the term is used.
- Define the sense used here, not a general dictionary dump.
- Prefer a plain word when a definition would not be needed.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "A mechanism is available for identifying specific definitions of words or phrases used in an unusual or restricted way, including idioms and jargon." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Define words used in a special way](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#unusual-words).
