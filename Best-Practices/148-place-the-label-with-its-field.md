# Place the label with its field

The label sits where the person can see which field it belongs to, including when text is large.

## When it applies

Use this for every field.

## The decision

- Put the label above the field when that avoids sideways scrolling.
- Put radio and checkbox labels where the person expects them, beside the control.
- Do not leave a label equally close to two fields.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Labeling Controls: "In general, placing labels above the form fields helps reduce horizontal scrolling for people with low vision and mobile device users." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Place the label with its field](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/labels/).
