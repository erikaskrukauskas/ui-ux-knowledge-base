# Pair sensory instructions with text

Instructions name the control in text. Shape, color, size, position, or sound is not the only way to find it.

## When it applies

Use this whenever help text tells the person what to press or where to look.

## The decision

- Name the control: "Choose Continue," not only "use the round button on the right."
- A position or a color may support the instruction. The name still stands alone.
- Color-only meaning is also covered by the non-color rule.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Instructions provided for understanding and operating content do not rely solely on sensory characteristics of components such as shape, color, size, visual location, orientation, or sound." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Pair sensory instructions with text](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#sensory-characteristics).
