# Do not flash above three times a second

Under the stronger flash criterion, nothing flashes more than three times in any one second. The threshold exception from the minimum criterion is not available here.

## When it applies

Use this when that stronger criterion is in scope.

## The decision

- Keep flashes at or below three in any one second.
- Do not rely on a below-threshold claim for this criterion.
- Confirm the current wording. The minimum flash criterion is a different page.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Web pages do not contain anything that flashes more than three times in any one second period." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Do not flash above three times a second](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#three-flashes).
