# Meet the text contrast minimum

Text and images of text meet the current contrast minimum against their background. Large text may use the lower ratio the criterion allows. Disabled text, decoration, and logotypes have the exceptions the criterion names.

## When it applies

Use this for text the person must read. Confirm the ratios on the current page before you treat a number as the rule.

## The decision

- Ordinary text currently needs at least 4.5:1. Large-scale text currently needs at least 3:1.
- Do not apply the text ratio to a logo or to text that is pure decoration.
- Component boundaries and state graphics follow the non-text contrast rule.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1, except for the following." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Meet the text contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum).
