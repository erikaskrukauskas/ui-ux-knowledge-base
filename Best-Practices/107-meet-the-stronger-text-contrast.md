# Meet the stronger text contrast

When the stronger contrast criterion is in scope, ordinary text and images of text reach at least 7:1. Large text reaches at least 4.5:1. Inactive text, decoration, and logotypes keep the exceptions in the criterion.

## When it applies

Use this when you are aiming past the minimum text contrast. Confirm both ratios before you treat them as the rule.

## The decision

- Ordinary text currently needs at least 7:1 under this criterion.
- Large-scale text currently needs at least 4.5:1.
- The minimum contrast page is the lower bar. This page is the higher one.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 7:1, except for the following." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Meet the stronger text contrast](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-enhanced).
