# Put the tab list before the panel

The tab list comes before the panel, where the person looks for it.

## When it applies

Use this for in-page tabs.

## The decision

- Place the list above the panel.
- Do not put the only list to the side or below, where it is overlooked.
- The panel should be obvious once a tab is selected.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Vertical and bottom list arrangements will cause users to overlook the tabs." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Put the tab list before the panel](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
