# Keep critical content on the selected tab

Content the task requires is on the tab that is already selected. Other tabs are supplemental.

## When it applies

Use this when one tab is shown by default.

## The decision

- Put the necessary content in the default panel.
- Treat unselected tabs as extra, because many people will not open them.
- Do not hide a required step behind an unselected tab.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Ensure that the content within nondefault tabs is supplemental rather than critical for a successful user experience." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep critical content on the selected tab](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
