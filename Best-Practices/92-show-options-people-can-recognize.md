# Show options people can recognize

Show the actions and the recent items. Do not ask the person to remember a command, a code, or a previous page from an empty field.

## When it applies

Use this when the person must pick a function, a product they already saw, or a next step.

## The decision

- Show the available actions.
- Show recent or saved items when the task is to resume.
- A startup tour that must be memorized does not replace labels on the screen.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 15 January 2024: "Recalling items from scratch is harder than recognizing the correct option in a list of choices because the extra context helps users retrieve information from memory." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Show options people can recognize](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/recognition-and-recall/).
