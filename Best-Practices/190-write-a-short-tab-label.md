# Write a short tab label

A tab label is one or two words and says what is in the panel.

## When it applies

Use this when you name a tab.

## The decision

- Use plain words the person would use.
- If the label needs a sentence, the choice is too large for a tab.
- Do not use all capitals. They are harder to read.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Tab labels should usually be 1-2 words." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Write a short tab label](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
