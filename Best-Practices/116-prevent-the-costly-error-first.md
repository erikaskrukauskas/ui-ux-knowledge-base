# Prevent the costly error first

Prevent the error that would cost the person the most, before spending the design on minor irritations. A message is the backup. The constraint is the design.

## When it applies

Use this when you rank which mistakes to design out.

## The decision

- Remove the condition that causes the high-cost error, or block it.
- Then reduce the small frustrations.
- Keep a confirmation only where prevention and undo are not enough.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 30 January 2024: "Prioritize your effort: Prevent high-cost errors first, then little frustrations." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Prevent the costly error first](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/).
