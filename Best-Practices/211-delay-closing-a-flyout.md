# Delay closing a flyout

A submenu does not vanish the instant the pointer leaves it. People need a moment to reach the items.

## When it applies

Use this when a submenu opens from a pointer hover.

## The decision

- Wait briefly before closing.
- Do not require a perfectly straight path into the submenu.
- Keep another way to the same items for people who do not use a pointer.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Fly-out Menus: "In addition, scripting is used to slightly delay the immediate closing of submenu items when the mouse leaves the area." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Delay closing a flyout](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/menus/flyout/).
