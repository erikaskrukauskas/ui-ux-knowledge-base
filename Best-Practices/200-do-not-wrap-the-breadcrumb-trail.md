# Do not wrap the breadcrumb trail

On a small screen, a breadcrumb trail stays on one line or it is shortened. A wrapped trail hides the task.

## When it applies

Use this when a trail is shown on a narrow view.

## The decision

- Do not let it wrap into several lines.
- Do not shrink the links until they are hard to tap.
- A single link to the parent is enough when that serves the task.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 1 September 2026: "On mobile sites, breadcrumbs can quickly wrap to multiple lines and eat up valuable space on an already crowded mobile display." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Do not wrap the breadcrumb trail](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/breadcrumbs/).
