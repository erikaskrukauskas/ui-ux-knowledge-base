# Offer a simple pointer alternative to a gesture

A multipoint or path-based gesture has a single-pointer alternative that does not depend on the path, unless the complex gesture is essential.

## When it applies

Use this for pinch, multi-finger, or drag-a-path actions the product interprets.

## The decision

- Offer a tap, a button, or another single-pointer control for the same function.
- Do not require the person to trace a path unless the path is the point of the activity.
- Confirm the essential exception before you omit the alternative.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "All functionality that uses multipoint or path-based gestures for operation can be operated with a single pointer without a path-based gesture, unless a multipoint or path-based gesture is essential." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer a simple pointer alternative to a gesture](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#pointer-gestures).
