# Let people resize text

Text can grow to 200 percent without assistive technology, and the person does not lose content or actions. Captions and images of text are the named exceptions.

## When it applies

Use this for body text, labels, and instructions.

## The decision

- Allow text to resize to 200 percent.
- Keep every action available at that size. Do not clip the label or drop the control.
- Confirm the current percentage before you treat 200 as permanent.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Except for captions and images of text, text can be resized without assistive technology up to 200 percent without loss of content or functionality." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Let people resize text](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#resize-text).
