# Keep the selected tab next to its panel

The selected tab sits against the panel it controls.

## When it applies

Use this when you place a tab list and its panel.

## The decision

- Keep them close.
- Do not insert a full-width rule, a large gap, or another object between the label and the panel.
- The person should see that the label and the panel are one group.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Another way to convey the connection between the tab label and the tab panel is to use proximity." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep the selected tab next to its panel](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
