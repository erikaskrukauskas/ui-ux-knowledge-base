# Offer another way out of a flyout

Items that live in a flyout are also reachable another way. A flyout is hard or impossible for some people.

## When it applies

Use this when a menu reveals a submenu on hover or on a pointer move.

## The decision

- Repeat those items on the parent view, or offer another path.
- Do not make the flyout the only way to the children.
- Keep the targets large enough to hit.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Fly-out Menus: "Make sure to provide other ways to reach the submenu items, for example by repeating them on the page of the parent menu item." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer another way out of a flyout](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/menus/flyout/).
