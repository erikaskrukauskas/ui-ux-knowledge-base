# Let people reduce motion

Non-essential motion that starts from a person's action can be turned off. Motion that is only decorative does not play when the person has asked for less motion.

## When it applies

Use this when an interaction moves, scales, or transitions something other than the essential movement of the task, such as scrolling the content they asked to see.

## The decision

- Leave out motion that does not carry the task.
- When motion remains and is not essential, honor a system or product preference for reduced motion, or offer a control that turns that motion off for the whole product.
- Keep the information and the function available when the motion is off.
- Motion that is essential to the information, such as previewing an animation the person is authoring, may remain. Say what is essential, and confirm that judgment against the current criterion.

## Evidence

W3C, Understanding Success Criterion 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions: "Support user preferences for motion, and eliminate unnecessary motion effects." The criterion says motion animation triggered by interaction can be disabled, unless the animation is essential to the functionality or the information being conveyed. Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html).
