# Keep breadcrumbs secondary to navigation

Breadcrumbs add a parent path. They do not replace the main navigation or the section navigation.

## When it applies

Use this when you add a breadcrumb trail.

## The decision

- Keep the global or local navigation.
- Use the trail to move up the hierarchy.
- Do not turn the trail into the only menu.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 1 September 2026: "Breadcrumbs are intended to supplement other navigational components, such as a global navigation bar." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep breadcrumbs secondary to navigation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/breadcrumbs/).
