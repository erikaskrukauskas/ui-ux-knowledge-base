# Make cancel quieter than submit

When cancel must exist, it is visually quieter than the action that completes the form.

## When it applies

Use this when a form collects sensitive input and people need a way to discard it.

## The decision

- Give submit the stronger treatment.
- Place cancel where it is not the easier target.
- Name cancel as leaving, not as another submit.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 1 May 2016: "But make sure that the Cancel button has significantly less visual prominence than the Submit button, to avoid accidental clicks." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Make cancel quieter than submit](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/).
