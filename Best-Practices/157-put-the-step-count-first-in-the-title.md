# Put the step count first in the title

The view title leads with the progress, then the step name.

## When it applies

Use this when a process is a series of views.

## The decision

- Put the step count before the product name.
- Update it when the step changes.
- People who hear the title first should hear the progress first.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Multi-page Forms: "Changing the title of the page to include the progress gives immediate feedback. This information should precede other information provided in the title." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Put the step count first in the title](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/).
