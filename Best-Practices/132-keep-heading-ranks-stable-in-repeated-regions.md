# Keep heading ranks stable in repeated regions

A sidebar, a footer, or another fixed region keeps the same heading ranks on every view.

## When it applies

Use this when a region repeats beside content whose headings change.

## The decision

- Do not renumber the repeated region to follow the article.
- Consistency across views matters more than a perfect local nest in that region.
- The article itself still nests without skipped ranks.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Headings: "In fixed sections of the page, for example in sidebars, the heading ranks should not change depending on the ranks in the content area." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Keep heading ranks stable in repeated regions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/page-structure/headings/).
