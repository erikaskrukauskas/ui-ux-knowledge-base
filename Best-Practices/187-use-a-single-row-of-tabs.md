# Use a single row of tabs

Tabs stay in one row. A second row makes the selection ambiguous and breaks memory of where a tab was.

## When it applies

Use this for a website or a simple app.

## The decision

- Keep one row.
- Do not stack a back row of tabs.
- If the labels do not fit one row, use fewer tabs or another structure.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 2 September 2026: "Websites and simple apps should avoid stacking tab lists within one tab control." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Use a single row of tabs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/).
