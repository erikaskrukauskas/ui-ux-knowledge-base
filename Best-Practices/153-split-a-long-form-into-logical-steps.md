# Split a long form into logical steps

A long form becomes a series of smaller forms, split where the questions already group.

## When it applies

Use this when one form is long enough to discourage people.

## The decision

- Split on real groups, such as shipping and payment.
- Keep each step about one group.
- Say that optional stages can be skipped.

## Evidence

W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, Multi-page Forms: "Split the form up according to its logical groups of controls." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Split a long form into logical steps](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/).
