# Leave out what the task does not need

Every extra unit of information competes with the task. Keep the view to what this step needs.

## When it applies

Use this when a view has accumulated asides, promotions, or repeated explanations.

## The decision

- Keep the content that serves the primary goal.
- Move rare detail behind a request, or remove it.
- A decoration that makes the next action harder does not stay.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 30 January 2024: "Interfaces should not contain information that is irrelevant or rarely needed." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Leave out what the task does not need](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/).
