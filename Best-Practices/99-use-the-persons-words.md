# Use the person's words

Name things with the person's words, in an order that matches the task. Internal system names stay inside the team.

## When it applies

Use this for labels, navigation, and errors.

## The decision

- Use the term the person uses for the object.
- Order the information the way the task unfolds.
- When a team term and a person term differ, show the person term.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, last reviewed 30 January 2024: "The design should speak the users' language. Use words, phrases, and concepts familiar to the user, rather than internal jargon." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Use the person's words](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/).
