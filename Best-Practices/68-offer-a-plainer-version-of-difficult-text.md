# Offer a plainer version of difficult text

When text, after names and titles are removed, needs more than lower-secondary reading ability, also offer supplemental content or a version that does not.

## When it applies

Use this for instructions the person must follow. Confirm the current education-level wording before you treat a grade number as the test.

## The decision

- Write the task in plain language first.
- When the text must stay difficult, provide a plainer version or a supplement.
- Confirm the current criterion. It is an additional requirement, not a ban on precise terms.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "When text requires reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level after removal of proper names and titles, supplemental content, or a version that does not require reading ability more advanced than the lower secondary education level, is available." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Offer a plainer version of difficult text](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#reading-level).
