# Reserve images of text for what must be drawn

Under the stronger images-of-text criterion, a picture of words is only decoration or essential to the information, such as a logo.

## When it applies

Use this when you are meeting that stronger criterion.

## The decision

- Replace styled-word images with text.
- Keep a logo as an image with a text alternative.
- Confirm the current essential exception before you keep another picture of text.

## Evidence

W3C Recommendation, WCAG 2.2, 12 December 2024: "Images of text are only used for pure decoration or where a particular presentation of text is essential to the information being conveyed." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Reserve images of text for what must be drawn](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#images-of-text-no-exception).
