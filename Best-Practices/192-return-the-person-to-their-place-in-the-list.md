# Return the person to their place in the list

Coming back from an item returns the person to that item, not to the top of a long list.

## When it applies

Use this when a list opens a detail and the person goes back.

## The decision

- Restore the position.
- Do not dump them at the top to scroll through everything they already passed.
- Pages make this easier because the set they return to is smaller.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 4 September 2022: "Frequently, users will click on an item in the infinite list or feed to go to its detail page and, when they come back, using the Back button, they will find themselves at the top of the list." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Return the person to their place in the list](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/infinite-scrolling-tips/).
