# Follow a familiar field order

Fields follow the order people already expect, and the most common choice comes first.

## When it applies

Use this when a form asks for a known sequence, such as a payment or a shipment.

## The decision

- Use the standard order for that kind of fact.
- List the common option before the rare one.
- Check that keyboard order matches that sequence.

## Evidence

Nielsen Norman Group, 1 May 2016: "Stick to standard sequences both for fields (e.g., Credit-card number, Expiration date, Security code) and for value choices." Confirm the current page before you rely on it: [Follow a familiar field order](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/).
